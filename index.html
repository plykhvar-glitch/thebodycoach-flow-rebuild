What this is

We opened The Body Coach the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at The Body Coach: installed it, went through every screen up to the first workout, read your App Store listing and your website, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people before they decide.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — your blue, your lime, the outline answer cards, Joe on the level cards, the kg / lb wheel, your paywall and your Login or Sign Up screen. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but show at the wrong moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
